Your data & the booking form

When you use the contact and booking form we collect only what we need to reply and to plan your tour — your name, contact details, the tour details and any rider information you give us. We use it to answer you and to organise the ride, and we do not sell it or share it beyond what is needed to run the tour.

You can ask us at any time what we hold, or ask us to delete it, by emailing wheelsonfireazores@gmail.com.

Cookies

This site keeps cookies to a minimum and uses them only to make the site work. Any embedded content — such as videos, reviews or an Instagram feed — may set its own cookies when you interact with it.

External links & embeds

Photos & media on tour

We often film and photograph our tours. If you would prefer not to appear in anything we share, just tell your guide on the day and we will respect that.
