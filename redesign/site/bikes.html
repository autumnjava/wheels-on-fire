Clash Ride Dirt
RockShox

The Clash has 170 mm of travel and 27.5" wheels, and is designed for one thing and one thing only — having fun. With a look of the FRS, the freeride affiliation is obvious.

In addition to descending at full gas, the Clash knows how to pedal to get to the trailhead when the lift is closed. Depending on the terrain you can optimise grip or dynamism thanks to a specific insert.

This is a really playful bike that puts a smile on your face on any terrain. It feels natural from the first ride, even if you only ride your home bike.

Size M — Can fit riders with heights from 1.63cm to 1.79
